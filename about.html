ABOUT ME

My name is Daniel Sankale, and I am a motivated and hardworking person who is passionate about technology and personal growth. I have recently completed my ICT studies, and this achievement has given me a stronger sense of direction and confidence for my future career.

What I enjoy most is learning how digital systems work and how they can make life easier, whether at home, in business, or in the workplace. I am curious, disciplined, and always willing to improve my skills through practice, training, and real-life experience.

Outside my studies, I am also a person who values responsibility, honesty, and consistency. I believe that success comes from dedication, patience, and the willingness to keep learning even when things become challenging. These qualities have helped shape my attitude toward both my studies and my future goals.

I am especially interested in fields such as ICT support, computer troubleshooting, software use, and digital problem-solving. I want to build a career where I can use my knowledge to support people, solve technical issues, and contribute to a modern, technology-driven environment.
